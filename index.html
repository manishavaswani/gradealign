GradeAlign

A grading-consistency analyzer for professors. Paste an assignment, generate a standards-aligned rubric (AACSB Assurance of Learning, AAC&U VALUE-aligned, or discipline templates), batch-score student papers with your own AI endpoint or by hand, then see exactly where the rubric's scores deviate from your grades. No account, no server — student work stays in your browser; text is sent only to your own AI endpoint, only when you click "Score with AI".

Interpretation

    AI settings optional

    Any OpenAI-compatible chat-completions endpoint. Endpoint, model, and key are stored only in this browser's localStorage and sent only to that endpoint. Everything in GradeAlign has a template/manual fallback.

    Project data

    The whole project — assignment, rubric, papers (including extracted text), grades, and scores — persists in this browser's localStorage. Export it to move between computers or archive a class.

    Privacy: student work stays in your browser. Extracted paper text is stored only in localStorage and is transmitted solely to the AI endpoint you configure, only when you run AI scoring. Rubric templates bundled with GradeAlign are original descriptors aligned to the AACSB 2020 accreditation standards (Assurance of Learning) and the dimension structure of the AAC&U VALUE rubrics — no published rubric text is reproduced. Sources and check dates are shown per standard in the data file. AI-generated scores are advisory; review them before relying on the analysis.
